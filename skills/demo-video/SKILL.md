# Skill: TrustLock demo video

## When to use

Recording or editing the 60–90s submission video, or drafting the X post.

## Rules

- Follow `docs/demo_script.md` beat for beat. Problem first, then lock, rider scan, failed attack, explorer/repo.
- 60–90 seconds. Free X upload is about 2:20; do not ramble.
- Voice: clear international / US English, not a stylised Nigerian accent (Timi's stated preference on the Sell-Safe demo).
- Label `demo` / `dry` / `live`. Never overlay a fake transaction hash.
- Show the injection failure: funds stay locked.
- X post: `docs/x_post_draft.md`, ≤280 characters, must include @NEARProtocol and #NEARHackathon.
- Do not post from this agent. Timi posts after he says yes.
